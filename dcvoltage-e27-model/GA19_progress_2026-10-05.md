## Part 1 — Product title as the H1 — ✅ Done

**Root cause found:** two separate bugs, both site-wide:
1. `templates/product.json`, block `text_GnyQiN` — the main title rendered as `<h3>`, not `<h1>`, on every product except 1025 (which had a one-off manual fix applied earlier).
2. `sections/product-information.liquid`, line 88 — the sticky add-to-cart bar duplicated the title a second time as `<h3>`, on every product including 1025.

**Fix applied:**
- `product.json`: `<h3>{{ closest.product.title }}</h3>` → `<h1>{{ closest.product.title }}</h1>`
- `product-information.liquid`: sticky bar tag changed `<h3>` → `<div>` (styling is fully class-based, no visual change), with a `{% comment %}` block documenting who/when/why.

**Verified live** (raw HTML, not just a visual check) on two different products:
- Product 1025: 1 real `<h1>`, correct title, duplicate `<h3>` gone.
- Product ~1156 (previously had 0 real H1s at all): now 1 real `<h1>`, correct title.
