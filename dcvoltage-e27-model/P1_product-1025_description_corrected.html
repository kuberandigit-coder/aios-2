A dimmable ST64 squirrel cage LED filament bulb in amber glass, made to be seen in open pendants, cages and wall lights. Choose 4W (450 lm, 2700K warm white) for a soft glow or 8W (800 lm, 2200K extra warm) to replace an old 60W bulb. E27 screw cap, 64 x 138 mm, CRI 90+, rated 25,000 hours. Use an LED trailing-edge dimmer. Indoor, dry locations only.

Installation & Safety

Switch off and isolate the electrical supply before replacing a bulb. Allow the existing bulb to cool before removal and ensure the selected bulb is suitable for the fitting's voltage and wattage requirements.

If using a dimmer, confirm that the bulb and dimmer are compatible. For electrical installations or modifications, use a suitably qualified electrician where required.
