2025 Total Cost and 2026 Total Cost — identical formula for both years: Sonya's own campaign cost (sum of google_ads.campaign_performance.cost for campaigns in the LEDSone Google Ads account's "Sonya" campaign group, group_name='Sonya', account_id=4503486236) plus her product-share of the "DM 46" campaign's spend (Pmax UK | Muguntha | Shoptimised | GB | DM 46 All | MCV | UK): the Sales-side attribution already gives Sonya credit for any DM 46 order containing one of her owned products, so her share of that campaign's cost is folded into this single Total Cost figure — SUM(google_ads.product_performance.cost) for that campaign, filtered to her owned product IDs (same list salesuk.js uses for Sales attribution). The DM campaign's full spend (across all staff's products) is not shown here and is not what drives Net/ROAS — only her share is. (Updated 2026-08-04 — 2025 previously excluded DM cost entirely; now matches 2026's treatment.)
Net = Sales − Cost. ROAS = Sales / Cost. Target Achievement (redefined 2026-08-10) = 2026 Net ÷ (2025 Net × 1.30) — i.e. this month's 2026 Net measured against a 30% year-on-year growth target on the SAME calendar month's 2025 Net (e.g. Jan 2025 Net £50k → Jan 2026 target £65k). 100% = exactly hit the 30% growth target; below 100% = fell short. Requires both years' Net for that month with 2025 Net > £0, otherwise N/A.
